Figure 4.3. Every zygote pooled into one normalised meridional frame: vertical is position along the polar axis (bottom vegetal, top animal), horizontal is distance out from that axis (left centre, right cortex). What is drawn is log₂(this gene ÷ the whole panel) in that frame — so it maps where the gene sits relative to every other gene. A map of raw density would just be a picture of the cell.
One value per zygote
The per-embryo log₂(animal density ÷ vegetal density) that the t-test is run on. The test is paired by construction — both halves come from the same cell — which is why five embryos can call anything at all. Scatter across zygotes is the quantity the P-value is actually about.
