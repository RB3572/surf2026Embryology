Pronuclear Structure Clock (3D)

From a static 3D zygote stack: audited segmentation (cell body · 2 pronuclei · polar body) → dimensionless geometry → a monotonic probabilistic clock calibrated on live-imaging trajectories. Returns a τ distribution with 50/80/95% intervals, QC/OOD badges, and 3D orthogonal exploration — not an unjustified point estimate.

Open model → 53 live-imaged zygotes · 2,057 frames

Pronuclear Distance Clock

Train a leakage-safe geometric clock on public live-imaging data (Scheffler 2021), validate it with embryo-grouped cross-validation, then apply the frozen model to this project's fixed zygotes to give each one a calibrated pseudotime τ with a 95% interval.

Sperm-Defined Division Plane

The same per-gene analysis as the Division Plane Sweep, but at a single plane fixed by the geometry: through the sperm, the polar-body centre of mass and the cell centre of mass. Per-side counts, a coin-flip null, permutation p-values, and the γ/μ density concordance.

Division Plane Sweep — every plane

Drop the polar-body-axis restriction: search every plane orientation through the cell centre of mass (~20,000 candidates at ~1°) and report each gene's single globally-best dividing plane, scored against a search-corrected null so a best-of-20,000 split isn't significant by chance.

Equatorial Division Plane

One plane per zygote: through the cell centre of mass, perpendicular to the animal–vegetal (polar-body) axis. Splits each gene's transcripts into an animal and a vegetal half, with per-side counts, a coin-flip null, permutation p-values and the γ/μ density concordance.
