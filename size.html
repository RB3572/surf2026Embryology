Size distributions by stage
Mean cortex radius is measured for every embryo at every stage — the distance from the centre of mass out to the cortex, averaged over ~650 directions, with the polar body and nuclei excluded. For a 2-cell embryo it is measured over both blastomeres taken as one body. 2-cell embryos additionally get the box that just contains both blastomeres, oriented along the axis joining their centres. Every embryo is included; nothing is filtered out. The dotted vertical line marks the embryo you have open.
Each curve is a Gaussian kernel density scaled to its own peak, the shaded region is mean ± 1 SD, the short dashed rule is the mean, and the ticks along the bottom are the individual embryos — so the smooth curve is never mistaken for the data. In the box panels, solid = height, dashed = length.
Every distribution on one axis
Figure 10.4, live. The same measurements on one micron scale, so the stages can be compared directly, with every curve scaled to its own peak — that is what makes the widths comparable when one distribution has 60 embryos and another 36, and it means height here is not a count. Zygote diameter (2r) is shown alongside the radius because it is the quantity comparable to a 2-cell box dimension. Note that length is well short of twice the height: the blastomeres press together and flatten rather than sitting as two free spheres.
